Features

Everything runs on your machine

¶

RAG Chat with Citations

Ask questions about your documents. Get answers with exact source references, page numbers, and passages.

✎

Markdown Editor

WYSIWYG editor with wiki-links, auto-save, and full Markdown support. Link ideas across notes with [[backlinks]].

🔎

Full-Text Search

Instant keyword search across all documents and notes with BM25 relevance ranking.

★

Thinking Partner

AI-generated mind maps and Socratic questions that push your thinking deeper into the material.

🎧

AI Podcasts

Generate two-speaker conversations from your documents. Listen to your research discussed like a podcast.

🔒

100% Local AI

Built-in model download. No API keys required. Your data never leaves your machine. Bring your own cloud provider if you want.

How it works

Three steps to start thinking

1

Install

Download the installer for your platform. Double-click. Done. No Docker, no terminal, no configuration.

2

Import

Drag in your PDFs, text files, and Markdown notes. NotebookLab indexes them automatically for AI search.

3

Think

Chat with your documents. Generate mind maps. Get Socratic questions. Transform notes into summaries. All locally.
