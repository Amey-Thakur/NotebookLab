NotebookLab

Your thinking partner,
on your machine.

Drop in your research. Ask questions. Get answers with exact citations. NotebookLab is an AI knowledge workspace that runs entirely on your computer. No cloud, no API keys, no data leaves your device.

Download Source on GitHub

v0.1.0 pre-release. Windows, macOS, Linux. Free and open source.

Everything you need to think deeply with your documents.

  • Chat Ask questions about your documents. Answers cite the exact page, paragraph, and source file.
  • Write Markdown editor with [[wiki-links]] and auto-save. Your notes link to each other and to your documents.
  • Search Full-text search across everything you've imported. BM25 ranking. Results in milliseconds.
  • Think AI-generated mind maps. Socratic questions that push your thinking. Connections you didn't see.
  • Listen Turn your documents into a two-speaker podcast conversation. Listen to your research on the go.
  • Own Local AI models, bundled. No API keys. No accounts. No internet required. Your data stays yours.

How it works

  1. Install

    Download one file. Double-click. No Docker, no terminal, no configuration.

  2. Import

    Drop in PDFs, text files, Markdown notes. NotebookLab indexes them for AI retrieval.

  3. Think

    Chat, search, generate mind maps, transform your notes. Everything runs locally on your hardware.
