NotebookLab is a desktop app that lets you import PDFs, notes, and research papers, then chat with an AI that has actually read them. Every answer cites the exact source, page, and paragraph. Runs 100% on your computer. No cloud accounts, no API keys, no data uploaded anywhere.

What you can do with it

Import documents, then use AI to understand them deeply.

Markdown Editor

WYSIWYG with [[wiki-links]], auto-save, and backlinks between notes.

RAG Chat

Ask questions. Get answers citing exact page, paragraph, and source file.

Thinking Partner

AI mind maps and Socratic questions that push your thinking deeper.

AI Podcasts

Turn documents into two-speaker podcast conversations.

Transforms

Summarize, extract key points, or run custom prompts on any document.

Local AI

Built-in model download. Runs on your hardware. Bring your own cloud provider if you want.
