## Why we built this

We wanted a place to think that belongs to no one but the person thinking. The
tools we loved kept moving online, behind accounts and subscriptions, where
every note travels through someone else's server. So we built the notebook we
wished existed: one that reads your documents, answers your questions, and
never sends a word off your machine.

NotebookLab is the first software we have released to the public. We hope it
grows. We hope people use it, love it, build on it, and let it earn a small
place in how they work every day. That hope is why every line of it is open.

## The Makers' Pledge

> We built NotebookLab with our own hands, and we stand behind every line of
> it. To everyone who trusts it with their thinking, we promise:
>
> Your work stays on your machine. No telemetry, no tracking, no quiet
> network calls.
>
> The source stays open, for anyone to read, question, and build upon.
>
> We ship nothing we would not use ourselves.
>
> When we get something wrong, we will say so plainly and fix it.
